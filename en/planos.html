FAQ · businesses

Payments, invoices and cancellation on business plans.

Can I pay for monthly plans with Pix?

Pix is accepted for the Diagnostic (a one-time payment) and, on the Dedicated plan, via a monthly invoice sent by email. Starter and Pro subscriptions use credit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, which support automatic recurring billing.

Do I get an invoice and a receipt?

Stripe sends a PDF receipt and invoice with every charge, using the company tax ID (CNPJ) you entered at checkout. Trustio issues the Brazilian service tax invoice within 5 business days of confirmation.

How do I cancel or change plans?

Through the customer portal (linked in your confirmation email) or at contato@trustio.com.br. Cancellation takes effect from the next billing cycle; there are no fees and no lock-in.

Does my payment information go through Trustio?

No. Checkout is processed by Stripe (PCI DSS Level 1). Trustio receives only the confirmation and the contact details needed to provision the environment.

Do I need the Diagnostic before subscribing?

It isn't required. It's recommended when the use case isn't defined yet — and the fee is credited against the first month of the plan you choose.

FAQ · for you

Billing, payment and what happens after the trial.

Which payment methods do personal plans accept?

Credit card, Apple Pay and Google Pay, on every personal plan — chat, Agentio and Combo. Pix isn't available yet: it's coming soon to the 7-day passes (one-time payment); monthly, 6-month and annual subscriptions stay on card. This answer applies to personal plans — business plans handle payment differently, as explained in the “For businesses” tab.

Does the monthly plan auto-renew?

Yes: it renews every month until you cancel, and cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you've already paid — no fee. The 7-day pass never renews on its own.

What happens after the 5 free questions?

The chat locks and nothing is charged. Your conversations stay in your account, and you choose a plan whenever you like — or simply don't. The 5 questions are once per account, not per month.

Is the WhatsApp and Telegram agent included?

The agent is now Agentio (by Trustio & Hermes), sold in its own plans — weekly, monthly, 6-month and annual — or together with the chat in the Combo, which costs less than both separately. Chat plans come with a 3-day Agentio trial, starting on the day you activate it. The agent answers only on your WhatsApp or your Telegram, on the number you register, never through a computer or terminal. After the 3-day trial, the agent continues only with an Agentio or Combo plan.

When can I use my paid plan?

Personal access is granted automatically after Stripe confirms payment. Use the same email address as your Trustio account at checkout. The 7-day pass starts at activation. To try it before paying, create your account, confirm your email and use your 5 free questions.

Do I have to give my phone number?

No. For the chat on the site, name, email and password are enough. A phone number is only needed if you want the WhatsApp agent, because it works on exactly that number.
