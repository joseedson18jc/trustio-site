Privacy

How we handle your data.In plain English, no runaround.

This page says exactly what Trustio collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and how you delete it all. If any line here doesn't match what you see in the product, write to us — the line is wrong, not you.

Last updated: September 22, 2026 · Data controller: Trustio · contato@trustio.com.br

What we collect, and nothing more

We collect three sets of data, each for its own reason. None of them is sold, rented or handed to third parties for advertising.

1. When you join the waitlist

  • Email — required. It's how we tell you access has opened.
  • Name — so we can address you by name.
  • Phone or WhatsApp — optional. Only to notify you faster on launch day. Leave it blank and you stay on the list all the same.
  • Interest, plan, industry, company and size — so we know which product to prepare for you. All optional, except industry when you sign up as a company.
  • How you want to get in — free list or pre-subscription. If you choose pre-subscribe, you get the payment link and early access by email; if you choose the free list, you are never charged.
  • How you plan to use it — optional, a pick from a list. It helps us prioritize what to build and changes nothing about your access.
  • The free-text “anything we should know” field — optional. It goes to the same inbox as the rest of your sign-up and is only read by our support team. Because it's free text, please don't put sensitive or health data, or other people's data, in it.

2. When you create an account

  • Name, email and password — these are your account. The password is stored as a hash: nobody at Trustio can read it, not even us.
  • Phone — optional. It exists for one reason only: to turn on the WhatsApp agent, which works on exactly that number. Without a phone, the chat on the site works just the same.
  • Usage record — how many questions you've asked, when you last signed in, your plan and the status of the 3-day trial. It's what makes the quota work and lets support understand your case.

3. When you chat

  • The text of your conversations — questions and answers are kept in your account so you have a history and can pick up where you left off.
  • Your conversations train no model at all, neither Trustio's nor anyone else's.
  • Trustio administrators can access conversations for support and to investigate abuse. It's not routine, and it's stated here so you know before you write, not after.

Where your conversations are processed

Your account, your sign-up details and the text of your conversations are stored in a Trustio database hosted in São Paulo, Brazil (region sa-east-1), with access restricted by policies in the database itself: each person can see only their own conversations.

To generate the answer, the text of your question and the recent conversation history are sent to the language model provider Trustio has contracted. This transfer is necessary for the service to exist: without it there is no answer. The provider in use, the country where it processes the data and the contract governing that transfer are disclosed in writing to anyone who asks, at contato@trustio.com.br. If the provider processes outside Brazil, that is an international transfer, and you have the right to know it before you write anything — which is why the information is given on request, not hidden.

For anyone who needs a contractual guarantee that nothing leaves the country, there's the private environment for businesses, where inference runs on dedicated infrastructure. See plans for businesses.

Who we share with

  • Language model provider — receives the text of your question to generate the answer, as explained above.
  • Stripe — processes payments. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never pass through Trustio's servers. We only get back the confirmation and the contact details needed to grant access.
  • Email service — delivers account confirmations, password resets and launch notices.
  • Cloudflare — serves the site and counts visits in aggregate, without cookies and without identifying anyone.

No one else. No social media pixels, no retargeting, no selling of lists.

How long we keep it

  • Account and conversations — for as long as the account exists. You delete it whenever you want.
  • Waitlist — until launch and for up to 12 months after, or until you ask to be removed, whichever comes first.
  • Payment records — for as long as tax law requires, even after the account is deleted. It's the only exception, and it does not include the content of your conversations.

Your rights, and how to exercise them

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) you can ask, at any time and without giving a reason, for: confirmation that we process your data, access to it, correction of anything wrong, a copy in a readable format, anonymization or complete deletion, and withdrawal of consent.

There's one way to do it, and no form: write to contato@trustio.com.br from your account's email address, saying what you want. We reply within 15 days. A deletion request erases the account, the sign-up details and the conversation history — and it can't be undone.

Security

Access to the site is always encrypted. Passwords are stored as hashes. The database isolates data per person at the database level, not just on screen — even if someone bypasses the interface, they can't reach anyone else's conversations. Model provider keys live only on the server and are never sent to the browser. No system is perfect: if we discover an incident that could put you at risk, we notify you and the ANPD.

About uncensored content

Trustio imposes no editorial filter on the model's answers. In return, responsibility for the content of the prompts sent and for how the answers are used lies entirely with whoever sends them. Brazilian law and the rights of others still apply: the absence of a filter is not permission for unlawful use, and illegal content may be investigated and the account closed.

Changes to this notice

If anything changes materially — a different provider, a different retention period, a different purpose — we update the date at the top and email everyone with an account before the change takes effect.

Any questions? Write to contato@trustio.com.br. We reply within 1 business day to general questions and within 15 days to formal LGPD requests.
