How it works

From sources to delivery, inside your perimeter.

Documents, archives and public sources come in, are read and organized into a private knowledge base. Agents work on that base and deliver the result for the team to review.

  1. Sources

    Courts, archives, contracts and case files.

  2. Reading and OCR

    Text and fields extracted and validated.

  3. Private base

    RAG with per-person access control.

  4. Agents

    They research, monitor and draft within rules.

  5. Deliverables

    Filings, alerts and reports for review.

All inside a private environment, with access logs and configurable human approval.
