Voice layer

Not every voice agent is built the same way.

The quality of an AI phone call is decided less by the script and more by the architecture behind it. There are three approaches on the market today, and the difference between them is audible to the customer on the other end of the line.

Comparison of voice agent architectures
Criterion Native speech ↔ speechgrok-voice · xAI High-fidelity synthesisElevenLabs Cascaded pipelineprevious generation
How it works A single model hears audio and answers in audio Text turned into directed voice, with its own identity Three systems in series: transcribe, decide, synthesize
Latency Under 1 s, end to end Depends on the text arriving first The sum of all three stages — the pause after the customer stops talking
Mid-speech interruption The model stops, listens and resumes Depends on the orchestrator controlling it Classic weak spot: talks over or freezes
Prosody and emotion Preserved — the audio never becomes text Controllable through voice direction Lost in transcription and artificially rebuilt
Brazilian Portuguese Native, among 25+ languages Strong, multilingual Varies with the transcription engine used
Voice identity The model's own voices Brand voice, consistent across channels Limited to the vendor's catalog
Independent benchmarking Speech↔speech model — TTS rankings measure only synthesis, not conversation v3 Conversational: 8th place, Elo 1210 on the Artificial Analysis leaderboard Not listed: voice integrators don't build models, they license other people's
Where Trustio uses it Real-time conversation: phone calls and WhatsApp voice Voice-overs, previews and brand voice It's what we're replacing

Why this decides the call. In a cascaded pipeline, every stage takes its time, and transcription throws away everything that isn't a word — hurry, doubt, irritation. That's why these agents sound correct and still artificial: they respond to the text of what was said, not to how it was said. In speech↔speech mode there is no text stage in the middle — and that absence, more than any script, is what makes the conversation feel like a conversation.

Comparison by architecture: the characteristics above follow from how each approach is built, not from opinions about vendors. Latency measured in the Trustio environment in br-sao-1.
