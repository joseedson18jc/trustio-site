What it does

Much more than answers.

You ask in plain language and Agentio gets it done: it researches, writes, summarizes, compares, and organizes routines and reminders. A few everyday examples:

💼

Career & Jobs

I can, for example: rewrite your résumé on my own, tailored to the job, fix your cover letter in English and scour the market for the ideal job — and even try to apply for you.

💰

Money & Shopping

I can, for example: watch airfare and tell you the moment it drops; compare 10 plans and tell you which one pays off; make sure you never miss a due date again.

🏠

Home & Routine

I can, for example: set reminders for everything (car service, property tax, gifts) and send you a full summary of your day at 7am: emails, calendar, weather and news.

🎓

Studies

I can, for example: turn 5 huge PDFs into a 2-page summary with flashcards, or explain any subject until you say “now I get it”.

🚀

Business & Website

I can, for example: build your company's complete website in a day — logo, copy, contact form — and then monitor what people say about it on social media.

✍️

Content & Social

I can, for example: turn a recorded meeting into ready-to-post LinkedIn content, a thread and a newsletter — with AI-generated images on the spot.

🤯

Boring chores

I can: fill in that endless form, cancel forgotten subscriptions and sort 2,000 photos by date and place.

⚖️

Smart decisions

I can, for example: compare 15 laptops or PCs and hand you a table with the best value and a link to the best deal.

🔒

Security

I spot suspicious apps with access to your accounts and tell you exactly what to revoke now — before it becomes a problem.

And sooo much more.

How to start

Three steps, nothing to install.

Agentio only answers the number you register. No new app: it's the WhatsApp you already use.

  1. Create your account

    It takes a minute. In the Trustio chat, enter your number and request the 3-day trial.

  2. Get a hello from Agentio

    As soon as the trial is activated, it sends the first message to your WhatsApp.

  3. Ask your way

    Type or send a voice note. It gets it done, tells you when it's finished and confirms before any irreversible action.
