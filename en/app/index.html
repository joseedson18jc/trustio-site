Skills

A skill is a folder of instructions that teaches an AI agent to do a task the right way. These 15 were picked and tested by Trustio's founder. Use any of them right here in the chat with one click, or download the .zip for the agent you use.

In the chat, the skill guides the conversation's answers until you change or turn it off. Scripts and files inside the .zip only run in your own agent.

How to install outside Trustio
  • Chat apps with Skills: upload the .zip, without unzipping it, in the Skills area of the settings.
  • Coding agents in the terminal: unzip into the agent's skills folder. Each skill sits in its own folder, with the SKILL.md inside.

The law, forensics and health skills are support tools: they do not replace a lawyer, forensic expert or doctor. A skill runs in your agent, with its permissions: read the SKILL.md before installing. The skills are written in the language shown on each card.
